### Baseline-vs-`C` differences (explained against the maps)

- **The baseline did not test the CUDA 13 libraries.** With the same
  `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`, the `ec1cac71` build loaded the system **12.6**
  `libcublas.so.12.6.1.4`, `libcublasLt.so.12.6.1.4`,
  `libcurand.so.10.3.7.68` and `libnvrtc.so.12.6.68`: its candidate lists
  try the unversioned name first (`libcublas.so`, …), the venv directory
  has no unversioned names, and the loader cache resolves them to 12.6.
  This is the cross-major hazard item 9 fixes (test plan § 1.2 predicts it).
  `C` loaded the venv's `libcublas.so.13`, `libcublasLt.so.13`,
  `libcurand.so.10` (10.4.0) and `libnvrtc.so.13`. So the baseline here is
  "upstream tests, 13000 bindings, CUDA 12.6 libraries", and it passed.
- `libnvidia-ptxjitcompiler.so.580.178.04` is mapped only at `C`: NVRTC 13.0
  emits PTX the driver had not cached, so it JIT-compiled it; the
  baseline's NVRTC 12.6 PTX was already in the driver's JIT cache from the
  G1 runs.
- **Host-dependent:** `test_pinned_copy_is_faster` failed in `C` main-1
  (`1.051046ms vs 712.7µs`, ratio 1.47) and main-2 (`1.064194ms vs
  711.243µs`, 1.50), the same near-1.5 ratio as in G1, where it failed once
  on each side. It is on the closed host-dependent list; the values are
  recorded, not reclassified.
