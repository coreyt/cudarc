## Probes re-run (design revision 3 evidence)

The FC8 root-cause probes (C, against `libcuda`, from the G6 diagnosis),
re-run in this session; output verbatim in the log:

- **M-2 (`m2.c`, one pool per process, 1 MiB requests):** caps 32, 64,
  96 → 32 MiB usable; 97, 128, 192 → 64; 193, 256 → 96; 512 → 192;
  1024 → 352; 4096 → 1376; each stops with `CUDA_ERROR_OUT_OF_MEMORY` at
  reserved = used = the usable size.
- **Attributes (`attr9.c`):** pool attributes 1–8 succeed; 9 and 10 return
  `CUDA_ERROR_INVALID_VALUE`.
- **Address-space budget (`slots.c`, `defer.c`):** a fresh process can
  create 4 uncapped pools; 3 after `cuDeviceGetDefaultMemPool`; 2 after a
  graph with an allocation node is instantiated (still 2 after
  `cuGraphExecDestroy` and `cuDeviceGraphMemTrim`); 3 while a destroyed
  pool has an outstanding 256 MiB allocation, 4 again after it is freed;
  200 pools capped at 64 MiB coexist with 3 uncapped ones; 43 pools capped
  at 8 GiB and 14 at 24 GiB fit.
- **Context creation (`ctx4.c`):** `cuCtxCreate_v4` with a zeroed params
  struct and `cuCtxCreate_v3(…, NULL, 0, …)` succeed;
  `cuCtxCreate_v4(&c, NULL, 0, dev)` dies with `Segmentation fault`
  (exit 139).
- **Free memory (`pp.c`, `cuMemAlloc`):** 96 MiB to 2 GiB move
  `cuMemGetInfo` and `MemAvailable` by 0.0–5.7 MiB while `VmRSS` rises by
  the full size; 4 GiB moves them by 1373 MiB, 8 GiB by 2934 MiB.
