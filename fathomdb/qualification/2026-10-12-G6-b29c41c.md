Observations, not dispositions:

- M-2 on the Orin (below): a pool with `maxSize` 64 MiB fails its 33rd
  1 MiB request, at reserved = used = 32 MiB (`limit=33554432`). On the
  RTX 3090 the limit equals the cap (64 MiB). `requests_within_the_cap_succeed`
  asserts limit + chunk > cap, which the Orin's 32 MiB limit violates.
- `cuMemGetInfo` free memory does not move when 96 MiB are reserved or
  released on the Orin (`free 52810706944 -> 52810706944`). The upstream
  `test_post_alloc_memory`, which asserts the same kind of `cuMemGetInfo`
  movement (`assertion failed: free3 > free2`), fails at the baseline and at
  `C` in all runs, so this is a property of the Tegra driver; the two fork
  tests that assert `cuMemGetInfo` recovery inherit it.
- The three tests that pass alone but fail in sequence fail on pool
  creation or allocation with `CUDA_ERROR_OUT_OF_MEMORY` after earlier
  tests in the same process.
- The test plan lists all six for G6 (§ 3.1, § 3.2). Which of them are
  Tegra limits (test plan change, "recorded Tegra limit" as for T-CAPTURE)
  and which are defects is an owner/design decision; this receipt makes
  neither call.
